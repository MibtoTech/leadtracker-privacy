Lead Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Lead Tracker ("the app") is published by Mibto Tech. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

The app stores everything you enter only on your device. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data, and the app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information you enter

You may enter details about your sales leads, such as names, company names, email addresses, phone numbers, deal values and notes. This information:

  • is saved in the app's private storage on your device;
  • is never sent to us or to any server operated by us or on our behalf;
  • cannot be accessed by us, so we cannot recover it for you.

Because you decide what to enter about other people, you are responsible for having a lawful basis to keep it (for example under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) in South Africa).

Backups and exports

  • Exports you make. The Settings screen lets you export your leads as a backup file (JSON) or a spreadsheet (CSV) and share it using your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes, such as email, cloud storage or another app, is your choice and is governed by that service's privacy policy.
  • Imports. Importing reads a backup file that you choose. The file stays on your device.
  • Operating-system backups. Your phone's own backup service, such as Google backup or iCloud, may include the app's data in device backups under your account with that provider. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. When you import a backup, you choose the file in the system file picker, and the app can only read that file.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual leads, or use Settings → Delete all leads to remove everything. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data from your device. Copies you exported or that are in operating-system backups are not affected.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version at the same address.
